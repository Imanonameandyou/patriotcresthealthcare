Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What’s the difference between private health plans and ACA plans?
A: The biggest difference is how they’re priced. ACA plans charge based on “community rating,” meaning everyone pays the same rate regardless of health. Private plans use individual underwriting to base premiums on your personal profile. This means healthy people can pay much less for same, or better level of coverage.

Q: Can I keep my doctor on a private plan?
A: In most cases, yes. The private carriers we work with have broad local and nationwide networks. We’ll make sure any plan we recommend includes your preferred providers.

Q: How do I know if a private plan is right for me?
A: The only way to know for sure is to run the numbers, and that’s exactly what we’ll do in your coverage review. We’ll get you real quotes, map them against your current plan, and determine your savings. If a private plan isn’t your best option, we’ll advise you to stay put.

Q: What if I have a pre-existing condition?
A: Not a problem. The private plans we offer are guaranteed issue, meaning you can’t be turned down or charged more based on health history. You’ll be asked some medical questions to determine your risk profile, but this is only used to identify the best-fit plan, not deny you coverage.
