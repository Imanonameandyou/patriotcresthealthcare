## The 6 essential buying beliefs
1. I believe that protecting myself, my family, and my financial stability from significant medical expenses is important enough that I need suitable health coverage.
The prospect must recognize that going without appropriate coverage or choosing carelessly can put household savings and financial security at risk. This connects the purchase to the deeper desire for security and family responsibility.

2. I believe that the monthly premium alone doesn't tell me whether a health insurance plan is actually a good fit for me.
I need to understand the bigger financial picture, including deductibles, out-of-pocket costs, coinsurance, provider networks, prescription coverage, and exclusions. A lower monthly premium does not automatically mean better value.

3. I believe that different types of health coverage offer different protections, and I need to understand exactly what I'm considering before choosing a plan.
ACA-compliant major medical, medically underwritten plans, short-term coverage, fixed-indemnity products, and supplemental coverage are not interchangeable. I need to understand their differences, limitations, and potential risks.

4. I believe that there may be health coverage options better suited to my particular budget, health circumstances, household, and needs—but I need someone to help me identify and evaluate them.
I shouldn't assume that the Marketplace is my only possible route, nor should I assume that a private-market alternative will automatically be cheaper or better. My actual eligibility, providers, prescriptions, coverage needs, and financial exposure must be evaluated individually.

5. I believe that Patriot Crest Healthcare and its properly licensed agent can help me understand my options honestly, explain the trade-offs, and put my needs ahead of a sales pitch.
I need to trust that the agent will listen, explain product types and limitations in plain English, verify relevant details, disclose important information, and allow me to make my own decision without pressure.

6. I believe that requesting a Patriot Crest Coverage Review is a worthwhile next step because it can give me the clarity I need to make a more informed decision, without obligating me to purchase a plan.
I don't have to figure everything out on my own. I can get help understanding the options available to me, ask questions, and decide what makes sense for my situation.
